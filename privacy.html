Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

Stargazer Games respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our virtual reality applications and games.

Information We Collect

Our games do not collect, store, or transmit personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address.

Game Data

Game settings, progress, achievements, and high scores may be stored locally on your device to support gameplay. Player name and scores are sent to a central store to show the highscores page.

Third-Party Services

Our games may be distributed through Meta Quest and other digital storefronts. These platforms may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies. Stargazer Games does not control data collected by these services.

Children's Privacy

Our games do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

Because we do not collect personal information, we do not maintain databases of user personal data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
